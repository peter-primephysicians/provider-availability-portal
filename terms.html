1. Program Description

Prime Physicians operates an internal SMS notification program to communicate open emergency department shift availability to credentialed providers at Rosebud Hospital (RBH) and Eagle Bridge Hospital (EBH). Messages include shift dates, times, and type, and allow providers to indicate availability by reply.

2. Who Can Participate

This program is limited to physicians, advanced practice providers, and staff who are actively credentialed or contracted with Prime Physicians. Participation is not open to the general public.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on scheduling needs. Providers may receive up to several messages per week during periods of high staffing demand. Messages are sent only during reasonable hours.

4. Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan. Prime Physicians is not responsible for charges assessed by your carrier for receiving SMS messages.

5. How to Opt Out

You may opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP to any message from our scheduling number. You will receive a confirmation that you have been unsubscribed and will receive no further messages. To re-enroll, contact us directly.
