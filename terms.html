Prime Physicians — Provider SMS Notification Service
Last updated: May 4, 2026

Service Description

Prime Physicians operates an internal SMS notification system to communicate shift scheduling information to credentialed emergency department providers at Rosebud Hospital ED and Eagle Butte Hospital ED.

Eligibility

This SMS service is available exclusively to licensed medical providers who are employed or contracted by Prime Physicians and who have completed written opt-in consent during the credentialing and onboarding process.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on scheduling needs and open shift availability. Providers will only be contacted when shifts relevant to their credentials and availability are open.

Message Content

All messages are transactional in nature and relate solely to shift scheduling. Messages include shift date, shift type (ABEM Day/Night, APP Day/Night, FMIM), and response instructions. No marketing content is ever sent.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive a single confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent. Reply HELP for assistance.

Standard Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Prohibited Use

This service may not be used for any purpose other than provider shift scheduling communication. Providers may not share, forward, or misuse scheduling information received via SMS.

Modifications

Prime Physicians reserves the right to modify these terms at any time. Continued participation in the SMS program constitutes acceptance of updated terms.
