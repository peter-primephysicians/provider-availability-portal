Prime Physicians · Rosebud Hospital ED

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 28, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Prime Physicians ("we," "us," or "our") handles personal information collected in connection with our SMS-based physician scheduling notification system used at Rosebud Hospital Emergency Department.

2. Information We Collect

We collect and store the following information from providers who participate in our scheduling system:

  • Full name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Specialty and facility assignment (RBH/EBH)
  • SMS message history (shift notifications and replies)

3. How We Use This Information

Provider contact information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Sending SMS notifications about available or open ED shifts
  • Receiving provider replies indicating shift availability or acceptance
  • Internal scheduling and staffing coordination

We do not use this information for marketing, advertising, or any commercial purpose unrelated to physician scheduling.

4. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell, rent, or share provider personal information with third parties except as required to operate the SMS service (e.g., Twilio, our SMS delivery provider). Twilio's privacy policy is available at twilio.com/legal/privacy. No data is shared for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Consent

Providers consent to receive SMS scheduling notifications during onboarding and credentialing with Prime Physicians. Consent is obtained verbally and in writing prior to any messages being sent.

6. Opt-Out

Providers may opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out, no further SMS messages will be sent unless the provider re-enrolls.

7. Data Security

Provider information is stored in a password-protected local application accessible only to authorized Prime Physicians scheduling staff. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access.

8. Data Retention

Provider contact and scheduling information is retained for the duration of the provider's engagement with Prime Physicians and deleted upon request or upon termination of the provider relationship.
