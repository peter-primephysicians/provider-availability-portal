Privacy Policy

Prime Physicians — Provider SMS Notification Service
Last updated: May 4, 2026

Who We Are

Prime Physicians manages emergency department staffing at Rosebud Hospital ED (RBH) and Eagle Butte Hospital ED (EBH) in South Dakota.

Who Receives Messages

SMS notifications are sent exclusively to licensed medical providers (physicians, nurse practitioners, physician assistants) who are employed or contracted by Prime Physicians. No messages are sent to patients or the general public.

How We Collect Phone Numbers

Phone numbers are collected directly from providers during the credentialing and onboarding process. Numbers are stored in a private internal scheduling application and are never shared, sold, or used for marketing purposes.

Opt-In Consent

Prior to receiving any SMS messages, providers complete a written onboarding agreement that includes an explicit opt-in statement: "I consent to receive SMS notifications from Prime Physicians regarding shift scheduling at Rosebud Hospital ED and Eagle Butte Hospital ED." No provider receives SMS messages without completing this written consent.

Message Content

All messages are transactional shift scheduling notifications. Content includes available shift dates, shift types, and instructions to reply 1 to accept or 2 to decline. No marketing, promotional, or advertising content is ever sent.

Opt-Out

Providers may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. They will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

Data Security

Provider contact information is stored locally in an encrypted scheduling application accessible only to authorized Prime Physicians staff.
